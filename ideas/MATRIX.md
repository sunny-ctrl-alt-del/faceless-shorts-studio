# Format × style matrix and the mixing rule (owner, 2026-11-11; v2 after 14 reference channels)

Owner's direction: no default style. Mix story formats and visual styles, only in combinations that make sense
(**✓ must try · ~ could try · ✗ doesn't work**), measure each by views AND retention, and shift the mix toward what
works. New formats/styles the owner shares get a new row/column here and enter the exploration pool.

## Mixing rule (the Sunday planner applies it; the calendar from 12 Nov follows it)
1. **Explore in two rounds (owner, 2026-11-12): 2 Shorts a day, 10:00 + 20:00 IST.** Round 1 (12-24 Nov) posts every
   ✓ combo once (Classic Cuts twice: one public-domain classic, one CC BY open movie), Round 2 (25 Nov - 7 Dec) does it
   again with each combo in the other half of the day (AM ↔ PM), so time of day doesn't decide the score. No format and
   no style twice on the same day or on consecutive days; every 7 days ≥ 5 formats and ≥ 5 styles. Plan:
   `tools/plan_slots.py` → `ideas/plan_2x.json`; ideas: `ideas/picker_v3.json`; `tools/build_calendar.py` writes
   calendar.md + `ideas/slots_2x.json`; Notion "Idea Picker · Round 1" / "· Round 2" (one row per slot).
2. **Then ~ combos** get one post each (one or two a week), mixed in with the best ✓ combos.
3. **Then exploit:** score = 0.5 × (views ÷ channel median) + 0.5 × (average view % ÷ channel median average view %)
   for Shorts ≥ 3 days old (Zapier YouTube "Get Report", Top Videos, metrics views + averageViewPercentage +
   averageViewDuration + likes + comments + shares + subscribersGained; `pipeline.sh scoreboard` for views/likes when
   the report isn't available). Each week: ~60 % of slots from the top third of combos, ~30 % under-tested, ~10 % new
   ideas. A combo in the top third two weeks running may take up to 3 slots a week; bottom third after 3 posts → "~".
4. Every Short ends with a story-specific comment question (RUNBOOK §2); comments are tracked per combo too.
5. Every Short gets a designed thumbnail (`references/THUMBNAILS.md`).
